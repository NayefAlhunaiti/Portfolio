Procurement Approval Policy

- All purchase requisitions above $10,000 require manager approval and a justification statement.
- Only approved suppliers from the company vendor list may be used for standard office equipment.
- Contracts longer than 12 months must be reviewed by legal before purchase order creation.
- Procurement requests that include IT equipment must be aligned with the technology refresh schedule.
- Any exception to policy must be documented with a risk explanation and an authorized approver signature.
